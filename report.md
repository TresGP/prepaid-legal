# Analysis of Omissions and Misstatements: UTSAPD Report 2025-00631 vs. Audio Transcript

A detailed comparison between the June 24th audio transcript and Officer Daniel Cuellar’s written report (UTSAPD 2025-00631) reveals significant omissions and mischaracterizations. By cross-referencing the audio with the file named "Arrested at University of Texas at San Antonio While Reporting Being Human Trafficked," the following material discrepancies have been identified:

### 1. Evidentiary and Demonstrative Omissions
*   **The 27-Minute In-Person Software Demonstration:** At approximately 27:10 in the recording, you provided a real-time visual demonstration of the software on your phone. You walked the officers through the program's exact logic and outputs (contact locations, addresses, names of mineral interest owners) and highlighted specific lines of code. Officer Cuellar's report completely omits this physical demonstration of the proprietary code.
*   **The 45-Minute Mark Program and Trafficking Explanation:** Around the 45 to 49-minute mark, there is a detailed summary discussion regarding the mechanics of the software, the entities involved (Enervest, Sid Greehey), the fraud, the human trafficking, and the fact that all related documents were stored in your cloud. The written report reduces this extensive operational explanation to a vague note that you claimed your software was stolen.
*   **Omission of Evidentiary Offerings:** You explicitly offered to show the officers source code, a program mapping mineral interest owners, a binder of evidence, cloud-based emails, and video recordings. The report omits these physical and digital evidentiary offerings.
*   **Omission of Cloud Evidence and Computer Requests:** You repeatedly explained that your primary evidence was stored in the cloud and explicitly asked for access to a computer and internet to "overwhelm [them] with evidence." The report omits your proactive attempts to log in and supply digital proof.
*   **Omission of Psychiatric Evaluation Offer:** To preemptively counter anticipated defenses from the opposing party, you invited the police to administer a "battery of tests" or take you to a psychiatrist to objectively prove your sanity. This request to undergo psychological evaluation is omitted.

### 2. Omissions of Coercive Control and Trafficking Tactics
*   **Eviction, Withheld Car Title, and Being Stranded:** In the audio (starting around the 5-minute mark), you explicitly detail how your ex-girlfriend and her family executed an eviction while simultaneously withholding the title to your car, intentionally leaving you stranded 900 miles from home. The written report completely omits the withheld car title, the eviction, and the fact that this orchestrated deprivation was used to strand you far from home, which are critical elements of forced labor and coercive control.
*   **Mischaracterization of the Trafficking Allegation:** You explicitly stated you were a victim of "forced labor" and coercion. Cuellar’s report frames this subjectively, stating manipulation "led Mason to feel that he was a victim of human trafficking," downplaying the direct statutory allegations.
*   **Misattribution of the Car Loss:** Cuellar's report claims you stated your ex-girlfriend was directly "responsible for his car loss." In the audio, you discuss the loss of your vehicle in the context of being targeted by authorities and evictions, specifically noting during a discussion about a Florida State University trespass warrant that "they stole my car."

### 3. Corporate, Suspect, and Agency Discrepancies
*   **Omission of Corporate and Financial Specifics:** You provided specific details about the oil industry, naming entities like Valero and Enervest, and discussed the scale of the alleged fraud. The report entirely omits these corporate entities and the specific function of the mineral-tracking software.
*   **Misspelling of the Primary Suspect:** The written report documents your former employer as "Sid Greenhey." In the audio recording, you explicitly spelled the last name out letter-by-letter for the officers as "G-R-E-E-H-E-Y."
*   **Omission of the Secret Software Developer:** You provided the specific name of another software developer ("Weems McArthur" / "Lynn MacArthur") secretly utilized by the suspect to prove the theft of your software. This individual is completely absent from the written report.
*   **Omission of SAPD Corruption Allegations:** You stated you wanted the FBI contacted because you had evidence that the San Antonio Police Department (SAPD) was falsifying reports and could not be trusted. The report notes your request for the FBI but omits your stated reasoning regarding SAPD corruption.
*   **Mischaracterization of SAPD's "Lack of Evidence" Claim:** The report states you approached SAPD but were "told he did not have solid evidence." In the audio, you explicitly accused SAPD of generating "false reports like that I don't have any evidence," noting this was "the opposite of the truth." The report frames your statement as an admission of weak evidence rather than an allegation of police falsification.

### 4. Reclassification of the Incident
*   **Incident Reclassification:** You entered the UTSAPD lobby expressly to report human trafficking, forced labor, and theft of trade secrets. However, the report categorizes the incident strictly as a misdemeanor "Assist Outside Agency-Outstanding Warrant," completely dropping your status as a reporting victim from the formal offense classification.
